the loop

One bankroll, one market, one winner.

01

Take a seat

Everyone pays the same entry and starts with 1,000 credits. Nothing else to deposit, and no real leverage: the only money on the table is the seat you bought.

02

The clock starts

The table moves to a faster layer built by MagicBlock. Orders land in about ten milliseconds and cost nothing, at the real market price.

03

The pit bites

Every few seconds the pit closes one player who is holding a position. Picked at random, with a draw anyone can check. Staying out of the market is a real defence.

04

The podium

At the bell everything is written back to Solana, the positions are revealed and the pot pays the top three: 60, 25 and 15 percent.

the twist nobody else has

When pressure builds, you lose control.

Getting bitten, closing at a loss and holding too long raise your pressure. At 100 you enter fever for 20 seconds: your orders run backwards. You ask for long and get short. The program really flips them — it is not a visual effect.

  • +25 you close a trade down by more than 20 credits

why it feels instant

Four pieces of MagicBlock, each doing a real job.

Fast layer

10 ms · no fees

While the round is live, the table runs on a MagicBlock rollup: about 10 ms per order and no fees. When the bell rings it all goes back to Solana.

Private mode

sealed for real

Your positions are sealed inside a private environment. Anyone outside the table asks for them and gets nothing back — checked on devnet, with and without a valid access token.

Verifiable draw

nobody can rig it

The player who gets bitten is chosen by a randomness oracle, and anyone can check the draw afterwards. A forged result is rejected by the program.

Real prices

live market prices

Prices come from Pyth and are read inside the fast layer, so you trade what the market is actually doing — not a number the house made up.
